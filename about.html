About Hakuna Express

Hakuna Express is a shopping-assistance platform designed to connect customers with trusted local assistants who can purchase requested items, provide updates, and coordinate delivery.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make local shopping more convenient by connecting customers with reliable shopping assistants.

  • Provide a simple and efficient way to submit shopping requests.
  • Help customers communicate clearly with their assigned assistants.
  • Support transparent shopping through receipts, status updates, and delivery confirmation.
  • Continuously improve the platform based on customer and assistant needs.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a dependable platform for personal shopping assistance, making it easier for customers to purchase products from local markets and receive them conveniently.

We aim to create a trusted community where customers and assistants can work together through clear communication, responsible service, and transparent order handling.

What We Do

Customers can create an account, submit a detailed shopping request, receive an assigned assistant, communicate through WhatsApp, track the order status, and confirm delivery.

Shopping assistants can view their assigned orders, purchase requested products, provide receipts, communicate expected delivery times, and update the order status through the assistant portal.

Our Story

Hakuna Express was created to simplify shopping for people who may not have the time or ability to visit local stores. The platform allows customers to submit requests and connect with assistants who can help complete those purchases.

The project combines account management, assistant assignment, WhatsApp communication, order tracking, delivery confirmation, and complaint handling in one application.
