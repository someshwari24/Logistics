Last updated:

By creating an account, submitting an order, accepting an assistant assignment, or otherwise using Hakuna Express, you agree to follow these terms and use the platform responsibly.

1. Customer Responsibilities

Customers using Hakuna Express agree to the following:

  • Provide accurate and complete information, including your name, delivery address, city, state, pincode, mobile number, shopping date, and requested items.
  • Review shopping details carefully before submitting an order.
  • Communicate respectfully with the assigned assistant through WhatsApp or any other approved communication method.
  • Clearly specify the required product, brand, size, quantity, price range, and any acceptable alternatives.
  • Confirm the delivery status honestly through the delivery confirmation page.
  • Select Received only after receiving the requested products.
  • Select Not Received or Problem with Assistant only when the chosen option accurately describes the issue.
  • Ask the assistant for the original receipt, bill, or a clear photograph of the receipt.
  • Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, card CVVs, or unnecessary sensitive information with assistants.
  • Not misuse the platform, submit false orders, provide false confirmation updates, or harass an assistant.

2. Shopping Assistant Responsibilities

Shopping assistants using Hakuna Express agree to the following:

  • Handle every assigned shopping request professionally, carefully, and respectfully.
  • View and update only orders assigned to their own assistant account.
  • Confirm product details, cost, quantity, brand, and acceptable alternatives with the customer before purchasing when clarification is required.
  • Provide the customer with the receipt or bill, either physically or through WhatsApp.
  • Clearly communicate the expected purchase and delivery time.
  • Update order status accurately through the assistant update page.
  • Not mark an order as delivered before the order has actually been delivered.
  • Use customer contact details only for handling the assigned shopping request.
  • Not share, sell, store unnecessarily, or misuse customer personal information.
  • Not request passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, card CVVs, or other confidential banking information.
  • Not request or collect payment through suspicious, misleading, or unsafe methods.

  • Hakuna Express does not request OTPs, bank PINs, passwords, or card CVVs.
