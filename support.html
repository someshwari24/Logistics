Please review item details carefully before confirming a purchase. Returns may not be available for items bought from local stores.

Frequently Asked Questions

Open the Sign Up page and enter your name, username, email address, and password. After successful registration, use the User Login page to access your account.
Log in to your customer account and open the dashboard. Enter the items you need, shopping date, delivery address, pincode, mobile number, and any other required details. Then submit the request.
After your shopping request is submitted, the system assigns an available assistant based on your order details. When an assistant is assigned, you can view their details and use the provided WhatsApp link to communicate.
Open the Track Order page from your dashboard or delivery confirmation page. Enter your order ID to view the latest status submitted by the assigned assistant.
Open the Confirm Delivery page after logging in. Select Received when your order reaches you. You can also choose Not Received or Problem with Assistant when appropriate.
Use the delivery confirmation page to report that an order was not received or that there was a problem with the assistant. You can also submit a complaint through the complaint option available in your account.
Return eligibility depends on the local shop and the type of product purchased. Some items may not be returnable. Confirm the product, quantity, size, brand, and price with your assistant before the purchase is completed.
Your information is used to manage your account, process shopping requests, assign assistants, and support delivery. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, or unnecessary sensitive information through WhatsApp or other messages.
Assistants should only be able to view orders assigned to their own account. They can update the delivery status of those assigned orders through the assistant portal.
